Dear SBF Baddie,

If you are going through a journey of heartbreak, rediscovery, and peace: Do not give up!

Grief isn't linear. Healing is a lifelong journey. Embrace it, don't avoid it. It's okay to grieve the loss of your companion and the loss of your intended future. But you can design a better one.

This can be a beautiful time of opportunity to rediscover who you are without the input of anyone else. Then, when or if you are ready, you can bring your best self into your new relationship and thrive.

Being Single, Bougie, and Free is a way of life. You will never regret the results of doing the work. I believe in you.

Love, Katurah
