Elysium Brain Dump

Session notes from June 1, 2026 — product scoping for the SBF premium portal.

Product identity

Working name: Elysium

Internal name for the SBF premium portal. A whole other realm — the version of this life where this is a full-time job. Think Inception: you click and step into a world where SBF runs at full scale. Not yet public-facing.

External brand candidates: Elysium by Kator · Kator's Elysium · The Elysium — A Kator Experience

Business model

Freemium + depth per category

Free tier gives real value — no woman leaves empty-handed. The newsletter is the teaser. Elysium is the full meal.

Paid tier unlocks the full hub per category — not just more resources, but deeper content layered together. A financial hub covers budgeting AND life insurance AND estate planning in one place. A legal hub covers tenant rights AND contracts AND small business law. Women pay for depth, not breadth.

Community forum stays free. Connection is the mission. Paid members need active, engaged people to talk to — a locked forum kills the energy.

Newsletter strategy

One newsletter · Two access levels

Everyone gets the same email. The "Go Deeper" link at the bottom of each section resolves differently based on Cognito tier.

Free users → hits an upgrade prompt ("This is a Premium resource.")
Premium users → goes straight into the full hub

One template to maintain. Free users always see what they're missing — that's the conversion engine. Same reason Spotify cuts a song at 30 seconds on free tier.

Digital products (future revenue layer)

One-time purchases

Templates (budget spreadsheets, lease checklists, negotiation email scripts) · Workbooks (30-day financial reset, solo life blueprint) · Mini courses (focused, under-a-weekend, actionable).

Women pay for a tangible thing they own — something to download, print, work through. Different psychological value than a subscription.

Bundle strategy

Premium membership + digital product = starter pack.

~$29 entry point: 3 months premium + one financial workbook. New member is now invested in the community AND has something in hand. Adds Stripe one-time payments alongside subscriptions — same setup, different payment type.

Auth & payments decisions

Social sign-in

Google + Apple to start. Facebook later if needed.

Apple required now — retrofitting it when the mobile app is built is painful. Lower friction = more sign-ups.

Stripe for payments

Web-first platform = Stripe. Industry standard for SaaS subscriptions. Google/Apple billing added alongside Stripe when the mobile app exists — that's normal, Spotify does exactly this.

AWS services in play

S3 + CloudFront

Host the frontend. CloudFront Free Plan activated 5/31/26.

Cognito

Auth + user groups (free / premium). Issues JWT tokens checked by Lambda on every protected request.

API Gateway

All request routing. Also receives the Stripe webhook on payment confirmation.

Lambda

All business logic. Moves users between Cognito groups when payment clears.

DynamoDB

Users, resources, subscriptions. Schema TBD — next session.

SES

Welcome emails, payment receipts. Custom domain @singlebougiefree.com confirmed.

Payment flow

User upgrades to Premium

User clicks Upgrade in Elysium
↓
Stripe Checkout (hosted payment page)
↓
Payment clears → Stripe fires webhook → API Gateway receives it
↓
Lambda moves user: free group → premium group in Cognito
↓
JWT token updated → user sees full hubs immediately
↓
SES sends welcome + receipt email from @singlebougiefree.com

What's next

Data model — next session

Define what a "user" looks like in DynamoDB. What a "resource" looks like. How the system knows what to show who. This is the dependency everything else builds on — Cognito schema, Lambda logic, resource gating. Nothing can be built until this is locked.
