Single. Bougie. Free.

An AWS-Powered Wellness Community for Women

Hey, I'm Katurah Bobo — a 4-year State Farm claims adjuster by day, and an aspiring Cloud Solutions Architect with a focus on FinOps by night. When I transitioned out of a long-term marriage, I found a lot of noise on social media but very few real resources for women who wanted to embrace their singleness rather than just survive it. So I built one.


Single Bougie Free is a wellness community for women learning to heal, pour into themselves, and thrive — not just wait for the next relationship. And because I'm a cloud nerd in progress, I used it as my hands-on AWS portfolio: real infrastructure, real pipelines, real data — built by me, for a cause I actually believe in.
