SBF Curation Framework

Your guide to curating meaningful newsletters for newly single women

Month 1: Foundation

Help newly single women establish legal, financial, and emotional basics

Legal
Protecting yourself legally after a relationship ends
Guidance Cues
  • Find resources about legal documents that need updating (beneficiaries, wills, power of attorney)
  • Consider resources on name changes, custody, or separation agreements if applicable
  • Look for plain-language guides (avoid legalese)
Specific Examples to Consider
  • "What Documents to Update After a Breakup" type articles
  • State-specific resources on legal rights
  • Trusted legal literacy sites (LawHelp.org, Nolo, etc.)
Mental Health
Processing emotions and building emotional stability
Guidance Cues
  • Find resources about grief and loss (breakups are grief)
  • Look for articles on journaling, therapy, or processing emotions
  • Consider resources on rebuilding confidence/self-worth
Specific Examples to Consider
  • Mental health articles from reputable sources (Verywell Mind, Psychology Today)
  • Journaling prompts or guided resources
  • Therapy finding tools (Psychology Today therapist finder, etc.)
  • Articles on "healing after breakup" or "processing loss"
Self Care
Practical self-care routines to stabilize yourself
Guidance Cues
  • Find resources about foundational self-care (sleep, nutrition, movement)
  • Look for simple, doable routines (not luxury spa stuff)
  • Consider resources on building sustainable habits
Career
Stabilizing your career and income
Guidance Cues
  • Find resources about career planning or upskilling
  • Look for articles on negotiating salary, asking for a raise
  • Consider resources on building professional confidence
Financial
Getting a grip on money basics
Guidance Cues
  • Find resources about budgeting for one income
  • Look for articles on emergency funds, debt payoff
  • Consider resources on long-term financial planning
Spotlight
Highlight a woman or resource doing something inspiring
Guidance Cues
  • Find a woman (influencer, author, creator, expert) doing something relevant
  • OR a tool/app/resource that's genuinely useful for single women
  • Should feel inspiring, not preachy

Curation Tips
Authenticity First
Only include resources you've actually used or deeply vetted. Your credibility depends on quality recommendations. Better to have 2 great resources than 3 mediocre ones.
Diversity of Sources
Mix different types (articles, apps, books, communities, experts). Include voices from different backgrounds if possible. Avoid always linking to mainstream sources.
Accessibility
Prioritize free or low-cost resources when possible. Always mention if something costs money upfront. Consider accessibility (transcripts for videos, alt text, etc.).
Currency
Check resource dates (avoid outdated advice). Verify links work before publishing. Update old resources if new ones are better.
Blurb Writing
Keep to 150 words max. Lead with the benefit to the reader. Be specific about what they'll learn/find. Use conversational tone (not marketing-speak).
"Life insurance, retirement accounts, bank accounts — if you named an ex as beneficiary, it's time to change that. This guide walks you through what documents matter, how to update them, and why it's not morbid to think about this now. 10-minute read that could save you major headaches later."
Personal Touch
Add a 1-sentence personal note if you've used it. Share why it matters to you or your audience. This builds trust and differentiates from generic roundups.
